# Learning Findings

## Confidentiality

Information should only be accessible to authorized users.

## Integrity

Data should remain accurate and protected from unauthorized modification.

## Availability

Systems and services should remain accessible to authorized users.

## Common Threats

Common cybersecurity threats include malware, phishing, social engineering, DoS/DDoS, brute force attacks, and insider threats.

## Security Principles

Least privilege, authentication, authorization, and defense in depth help reduce security risks.
